What We Stand For

Every product we build follows these principles.

🔐

Zero Knowledge

Your data is encrypted before it leaves your device. We cannot read it, even if we wanted to. Your encryption key is your password — and we never store it.

🚫

No Ads, No Tracking

We will never show you ads, track your behavior, or sell your data to third parties. Our revenue comes from subscriptions — you are our customer, not our product.

🏠

Built for Families

Our tools are designed for real households — multiple members, multiple accounts, shared expenses. Because managing money is a family activity.

💡

Simple by Design

No complicated setup, no bank connections required. Import a CSV, see your spending. We believe powerful tools don't need to be complex.

🔓

Transparent

Clear pricing, honest policies, no dark patterns. Our privacy policy, terms of service, and deletion process are all publicly accessible — no surprises.

🇺🇸

US-Based

Nudge Labs LLC is a Georgia company. Your data is hosted on AWS in the US with standard enterprise-grade security and redundancy.
